Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email chalkstrengthsupport@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

My data is missing on a new iPhone

Chalk Strength syncs through your iCloud account. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID and iCloud is on, then open the app and give it a minute to sync.

How do I back up my history?

Open Settings > Data & Setup > Export history as CSV, then save the file to Files or send it to yourself. The CSV is a personal record; the app cannot import it back.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Settings > Data & Setup > Delete all data. This permanently erases your maxes, history and settings from your iPhone and from iCloud. Deleting the app alone does not remove your synced data.

I did not get a notification when my rest timer ended

Check that notifications are allowed for Chalk Strength in iPhone Settings > Notifications, and that Rest timer is on in the app under Settings > Gym Setup. Notifications only arrive while a rest timer is running.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings > Full Program > Restore purchases, with the same Apple Account you bought with. Restoring is free and works on any of your devices. If it can’t find your purchase, email the address below and include your order number from Apple’s receipt.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the Chalk Strength purchase.

How do I change my maxes or units?

Maxes are in Settings > Maxes. Pounds or kilograms is in Settings > App Appearance, and switching converts your maxes, records and prescribed weights.
