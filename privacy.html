Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Chalk Strength has no accounts, ads or tracking. Your training data stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. I never receive it.

Chalk Strength (“the app”) is a strength-training program tracker published by Thomas Rehnert (“I”). This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

Information the app handles

You enter this yourself, and it is stored on your device:

  • Your first name or nickname (optional)
  • Your units (pounds or kilograms) and preferences, such as light or dark mode and rest-timer settings
  • Your one-rep max numbers for squat, bench press, deadlift and overhead press
  • Your workout history: sets, weights, reps, completed and missed sets, and any notes you type
  • Your position in the training program

The app does not ask for or collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifier or health-record data.

Where it is stored

On your device. The data above is saved in the app’s local storage on your iPhone.

In iCloud (automatic). If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, the app syncs the data above using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so it can appear on your other Apple devices and be restored if you reinstall the app. This data lives in your iCloud account and is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. I cannot read it. To stop syncing, sign out of iCloud on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification on your device to tell you when your rest timer ends. It is created and delivered on your device. It does not go through any server, and I receive no information about it. You can turn it off any time in Settings > Notifications > Chalk Strength.

Exporting your data

The app can export your workout history as a CSV file. This happens only when you tap Export, and the file goes wherever you choose in the iOS share sheet (for example Files, AirDrop or Mail). I do not receive it.

Network use and third parties

The app does not send your training data to any server that I operate. It has no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tracking tools. The app’s typefaces are included inside the app, so it does not contact any server to load fonts or other content.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Data synced through iCloud stays in your iCloud account, and deleting the app does not remove it. You can erase all of your data at any time in the app under Settings > Data & Setup > Delete all data. This removes it from your device and from your iCloud sync storage, and any other device signed in to the same iCloud account clears its copy the next time it opens the app. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Because I do not hold a copy of your data, I cannot retrieve or delete it for you.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Your data is protected by the security of your device and your iCloud account. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if accounts or online features are added, I will update this policy and change the effective date above.
